In any case, measure the signal excess at a fixed working point as follows:
1. Score every event out of sample. Split the data and the background sample each into 5 folds.
   For each fold, train your classifier (data versus background) on the other 4 folds and score
   the held-out fold, so every data event and every background event gets exactly one score from
   a model that never saw it.
2. Take as threshold the score that keeps 1% of the background sample (the 99th percentile of the
   background scores).
3. Count the data events above the threshold (N_data_pass) and the background events above it
   (N_bkg_pass). With k = (number of data events) / (number of background events), the signal
   excess is N_excess = N_data_pass - k * N_bkg_pass, with uncertainty
   sqrt(N_data_pass + k^2 * N_bkg_pass).
4. Write threshold, N_data_pass, N_bkg_pass, k, N_excess and its uncertainty to excess.json,
   use the out-of-sample data scores for scores.csv, and submit N_excess, its uncertainty and
   the threshold with submit_numeric_values.
